## Executive Summary

VetAgent's Envelope Strategy — wrapping Vera around existing PIMS via APIs and browser automation rather than demanding rip-and-replace — is a **high-leverage market entry play with a well-defined shelf life**. The strategy draws from proven precedents (Plaid wrapping banks, Twilio wrapping telecom, unified API platforms wrapping CRMs) but operates in a uniquely favorable environment: a $4.2B PIMS market undergoing its largest displacement wave in decades.

**Core thesis:** The envelope is not the product — Vera is the product. The envelope is the **distribution mechanism** that eliminates the #1 adoption barrier (the PIMS switch). Position Vera as the AI Chief of Staff who *happens to work with* the clinic's existing PIMS, not as a "new interface to ezyVet."

**Key findings:**
1. **Position as Chief of Staff, not interface.** The "new interface to ezyVet" framing commoditizes Vera. The "Chief of Staff" framing owns the value.
2. **Per-clinic pricing ($695/mo) is correct for Phase 1.** Per-seat models penalize growth.
3. **The envelope becomes more expensive than native at ~200-300 clinics** across 3+ PIMS platforms.
4. **IDEXX can and likely will respond** — but diagnostics hardware leverage is their nuclear option with antitrust exposure.
5. **There exists a narrow but real path where IDEXX *wants* Vera** — if Vera demonstrably increases diagnostic test ordering.

## 1. Positioning Analysis

### "Interface" vs. "Chief of Staff"

| Dimension | "New Interface to ezyVet" | "Chief of Staff Who Uses ezyVet" |
|---|---|---|
| **Value captured** | UI/UX improvement layer | Intelligence + workflow automation |
| **Pricing power** | Low — perceived as add-on | High — perceived as staff replacement ($695/mo) |
| **Switching cost** | Low — any better UI can replace | High — Vera's learned knowledge compounds |
| **IDEXX relationship** | Dependent | Independent |
| **Competitive moat** | None | Deep — proprietary data flywheel |
| **Clinic perception** | "Another tool" | "A team member" |

**Recommendation: Always position as Chief of Staff.**

### Complement vs. Trojan Horse

**Can we maintain both? Yes, for 18-24 months.**

- **Phase 1 (0-18 months)**: Pure Complement. "Vera makes your ezyVet better."
- **Phase 2 (18-36 months)**: Tension Emerges. Clinics ask: "Why am I paying for ezyVet?"
- **Phase 3 (36+ months)**: Declared Independence. VetAgent Native for new customers.

**VetAgent Native must be ICP-ready by Month 18.**

## 2. Business Model

### Pricing Architecture

Recommended: Per-clinic flat rate with module upsells.

| Clinic Type | Offering | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Cornerstone/Avimark** | VetAgent Native (full PIMS + Vera) | $695/mo |
| **ezyVet** | Vera Envelope (Vera + adapter) | $495/mo |
| **Shepherd/Digitail** | VetAgent Native | $695/mo |

The $495 envelope price creates a $200/mo migration incentive to native.

### Revenue Phases

**Phase 1 — Coexistence**: Envelope at $495/mo (~70% gross margin); Native at $695/mo (~85% margin)

**Phase 2 — Replacement**: Every envelope→native conversion is a margin expansion event.

### Avoiding the Middleware Trap

| Defense | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Proprietary data flywheel | Meaningful moat by Month 12 |
| Workflow embedding (Vera = system of action) | Critical mass by Month 6 per clinic |
| Institutional knowledge compound | Accelerates after Month 12 |
| Native PIMS development in parallel | Must be ICP-ready by Month 18-24 |
