## Executive Summary

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The envelope strategy is viable as a time-limited bridge — not as a destination.** All five perspectives converge on this conclusion: wrap ezyVet to enter the market fast, but build toward VetAgent Native as the durable product. The window is **12–18 months** before IDEXX's counter-moves become operationally devastating. Every week spent on the envelope that doesn't simultaneously advance native capabilities is borrowed time.

### The Three Truths All Perspectives Agree On

1. **The envelope is not the product — Vera is the product.** The PIMS adapter is distribution infrastructure. Vera's intelligence, institutional learning, and workflow automation are the value. Position accordingly: "Chief of Staff," never "interface."

2. **IDEXX will respond.** Not if — when. AI-Assisted SOAP Notes are already live. Patient Summarization is in beta. IDEXX is hiring for "agentic systems." The August 2026 Investor Day will likely announce accelerated AI investment under new CEO Mike Erickson.

3. **The ezyVet ToS is a live grenade.** Browser automation violates ezyVet's terms of service. The Private Integration Agreement is a revocable license. This is the single most important risk to address before scaling.

## Open Questions for Leadership

These questions emerged across all perspectives and require founder-level decisions:

### Strategic

1. **Partnership or stealth?** Pursuing ezyVet integration partnership reveals strategy to IDEXX but provides legal protection and stable API access. 23 clinics generating API traffic will be visible regardless. *Recommendation from all perspectives: pursue partnership.*

2. **What's the exit strategy?** Is this a build-to-IPO play (needs $10M+ ARR and platform independence) or a build-to-acquire play (IDEXX buys VetAgent for $30–80M)? The answer changes everything about how aggressively to build native vs. envelope.

3. **Should we lead with Cornerstone/Avimark displacement instead of ezyVet envelope?** The 25,000+ displaced practices are higher-volume, higher-margin, and zero IDEXX risk. The ezyVet envelope validates multi-location and AI orchestration, but native displacement is the bigger revenue engine.

### Tactical

4. **What percentage of Vera's value requires write access to ezyVet?** If 80%+ of value is deliverable read-only + direct client communication (SMS/email outside ezyVet), the risk profile changes dramatically.

5. **What happens if ezyVet actively recruits Goldsmith?** IDEXX could offer significant discounts or premium features to the 23-clinic group. What's the retention strategy? Advisory equity? Contractual lock-in?

6. **Should Vera offer diagnostics-agnostic positioning?** "Works with IDEXX *and* Antech *and* Zoetis" is a differentiator IDEXX-owned software can never match. Is this worth building?

### Technical

7. **Can we use ezyVet's Data Lake for read operations?** This could eliminate rate limit concerns entirely for read-heavy workloads.

8. **Does ezyVet's existing "AI-Assisted Notes" conflict with Vera's SOAP writes?** Two AI systems writing to the same clinical record is a recipe for data integrity issues.

### Legal

9. **Has anyone explored whether clinic-authorized browser automation is legally distinguishable from unauthorized scraping?** The Plaid precedent suggests "acting on behalf of the user with their credentials and consent" may be defensible, but ezyVet's ToS is more explicit than bank ToS was.

10. **What is VetAgent's malpractice insurance position?** If Vera's risk scoring downgrades an emergency and an animal dies, who is liable? Standard SaaS indemnification is almost certainly insufficient.

## The Bottom Line

The envelope strategy works **if and only if** it is treated as a bridge with a hard expiration date.

**The bridge buys us:**
- Market entry without demanding PIMS switch (eliminates #1 adoption barrier)
- Goldsmith pilot validation (23 clinics, real clinical workflow data)
- Data flywheel kickstart (practice-specific institutional intelligence)
- Revenue while building native (positive unit economics from Day 1)

**The bridge expires when:**
- IDEXX ships competing features (70% probability within 24 months)
- Adapter maintenance exceeds 25% of engineering budget
- VetAgent Native is ready for the ICP (target: 18 months)
- Clinics start asking "why am I paying for ezyVet?" (inevitable at 60%+ workflow coverage)

**The three things that must be true for this to work:**
1. The legal foundation is solid (ToS review + partnership application — Day 1 actions)
2. Engineering time is split ~60/40 between native and envelope (not 100% envelope)
3. The data flywheel is generating proprietary value by Month 6

If all three are true, the envelope strategy provides a 12–18 month window to build something IDEXX cannot replicate: a multi-PIMS, practice-specific, AI-native veterinary operating system with Vera at its core.

If any one of them is false, we need to know within 30 days and restructure accordingly.
