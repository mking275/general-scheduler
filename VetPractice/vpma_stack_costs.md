# 🐾 Veterinary Practice Companion Software Stack — Cost Model Report
**Research Date:** June 2026 | **Confidence Scale:** HIGH / MED / LOW

---

## Executive Summary

A typical independent veterinary practice running 2–3 veterinarians spends between **$1,200–$2,800/month** on companion software tools layered on top of their core PIMS — tools for client communication, telemedicine, analytics, inventory, payroll, and marketing. These tools are almost universally siloed, lack deep PIMS integration, and arrive with annual contracts and price opacity. The largest single spend category is **client communications/texting ($200–$500/mo)** followed by **reputation/marketing ($300–$500/mo)** and **payroll ($100–$200/mo)**. Inventory and analytics tools skew toward enterprise practices. VPMA's unified platform with MODs would consolidate most of these costs into a single contract.

**Source:** petdesk.com/pricing, G2, Capterra, Software Advice | **Confidence:** MED

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Listed Price** | ~$249/mo starting (not publicly posted; quote-based) |

## KEY STRATEGIC FINDINGS

### Biggest Displacement Opportunity
The **$300–$500/mo client communications slot** (PetDesk/VitusVet) is the clearest win. These tools are expensive, poorly integrated, universally complained about. VPMA native comms = **immediate $300–$500/mo savings, visible and quantifiable**.

### Universal Pain Points Across All Tools
1. **Annual contract lock-in** — Podium, Birdeye, PetDesk all require 12-month contracts with auto-renewal
2. **PMS sync failures** — Every comm tool has PIMS sync complaints; two-way data flow is broken universally
3. **Price opacity** — No major vet companion tool publishes transparent pricing
4. **Tool sprawl** — Average mid-size practice logs into 5–8 separate systems daily
5. **Hidden fees** — Add-ons, 10DLC SMS fees, extra location fees, renewal escalation

### What VPMA Should NOT Build
- **Payroll natively** — Gusto/ADP are best-in-class; VPMA should integrate, not compete
- **Ordering marketplace** — Vetcove is free and beloved; integrate it, don't replace it
- **Google Ads management** — Out of scope; recommend iVET360 or VetMatrix as partners

### Key Quotes for Marketing Use
> *"We were paying $480/month and didn't realize half the features needed another add-on."* — Capterra, PetDesk

> *"Podium's contract is brutal. We wanted to leave at month 10 and they held us to the full 12 months plus auto-renewed."* — Gartner, Podium
