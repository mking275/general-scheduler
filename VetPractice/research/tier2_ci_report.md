# Tier 2 Competitor CI Report

## Overview

This report covers four Tier 2 competitors in the veterinary PIMS market: Covetrus Pulse, Digitail, Vetspire, and DaySmart Vet.

---

## Covetrus Pulse

### Summary

Enterprise 'veterinary operating system'; best for mid-to-large practices in the Covetrus ecosystem. Quote-only pricing.

### Key Differentiators

- Native AI Scribe (ambient SOAP); claims 6 hrs/week saved
- 250+ integrations
- Built-in controlled substance (CS) log
- QuickBooks + GapOnly insurance
- IDEXX/Antech native two-way integration
- Two-way texting via Covetrus Comms

### Pricing

- Quote-only; no public pricing page

### Target Market

Mid-to-large practices; existing Covetrus supply customers; Avimark migration targets

### Weaknesses

- Performance instability/slowdowns
- Too-many-clicks UX
- Poor reporting UX
- Difficult implementation

---

## Digitail

### Summary

AI-native challenger with modern UX. Starting ~$289/vet/month. Most agentic of the four Tier 2 competitors.

### Key Differentiators

**Tails AI Suite:**
- **Tails Concierge** — front desk AI agent (most agentic of the four)
- **Tails Medical** — AI SOAP + charge capture
- **Tails Practice Manager** — analytics coaching

**Platform:**
- Native IDEXX + Antech two-way integration
- Pet Parent mobile app
- Open API
- Native telemedicine
- 4.7/5 Capterra rating

### Pricing

- Starting ~$289/vet/month

### Target Market

Modernizing independent and small-group practices; practices prioritizing AI and UX

### Weaknesses

- Learning curve for new users
- Some features still maturing
- Occasional post-update bugs

---

## Vetspire

### Summary

Developer-friendly, specialty/multi-location enterprise PIMS. Quote-only pricing. Best-in-class open API (GraphQL).

### Key Differentiators

- **GraphQL API** — best-in-class open API in the PIMS market
- **AI Scribe** — iOS app, ambient SOAP
- **AI Patient Summary** — 3-year history synthesis
- **Specialty apps:** WINK + SMILE
- Strong multi-location dashboard
- Trupanion direct integration
- CUBEX + VetSnap integrations
- Compliance dashboard

### Pricing

- Quote-only; no public pricing page

### Target Market

Specialty hospitals, multi-location enterprises, developer-heavy practices, enterprise DSOs

### Weaknesses

- Bugs at launch
- Mixed support reviews
- No public pricing
- Telemedicine via integration only (not native)

---

## DaySmart Vet

### Summary

Best value option for independent small-to-mid practices. 2,500+ practices. Starts ~$116/month. Strong pricing transparency.

### Key Differentiators

- **Daisy Voice AI** — voice-to-SOAP; 50% faster documentation; paid add-on $99/month
- Best pricing transparency in the market
- 24/7 support
- PetCare mobile app
- 40+ integrations
- Native online booking

### Pricing

- Starting ~$116/month (base)
- Daisy Voice AI: +$99/month add-on

### Target Market

Budget-conscious independent practices; 1–3 DVM; first-time PIMS switchers

### Weaknesses

- No native CS log (VetSnap required)
- No native telemedicine
- Add-on fees frustrate users (AI is not included)

## AI Ranking (Most to Least Agentic)

| Rank | Competitor | AI Product | Agentic Level | Notes |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| 1 | **Digitail** | Tails Concierge + Tails Medical + Tails Practice Manager | 🟢 Most Agentic | Tails Concierge is a true front-desk AI agent; most autonomous of all four |
| 2 | **Vetspire** | AI Scribe + AI Patient Summary | 🟡 Moderately Agentic | Multi-context AI (SOAP + history synthesis); strong but reactive |
| 3 | **Covetrus Pulse** | AI Scribe | 🟡 Moderate | Ambient SOAP only; 6 hrs/week saved claim; 250+ integrations amplify value |
| 4 | **DaySmart Vet** | Daisy Voice | 🔴 Least Agentic | Voice-to-SOAP only; paid add-on; no autonomous capabilities |

### AI Notes

- **Digitail** is the only Tier 2 competitor with a meaningfully agentic AI product (Tails Concierge handles front-desk workflows autonomously)
- **Vetspire** and **Covetrus Pulse** offer strong AI SOAP tools but remain reactive/advisory
- **DaySmart Vet** offers AI documentation assistance at lowest cost but zero agentic capability
- None of the four Tier 2 competitors offer: AI waitlist backfill, automated patient risk scoring, agentic follow-up pipelines, or agent audit logs
