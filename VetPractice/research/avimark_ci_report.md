# Avimark CI Report

## Executive Summary

Avimark is a legacy on-premise Windows server PIMS with ~11,000+ installs in North America. Owned by Covetrus; actively being migrated to Covetrus Pulse. Sunset risk is HIGH. G2 rating: 4.1/5 (87 reviews). Pricing is quote-based (per-location + per-workstation). All 10 AI/Agentic features score ❌.

## Top 3 Strengths

1. **Lab integrations** — deep two-way integrations with IDEXX, Antech, Heska, Vetscan; best-in-class lab connectivity across the board
2. **Financial reporting accuracy** — consistently top-praised by long-tenured users; robust native reporting and reconciliation
3. **Broad third-party ecosystem** — decades of integrations; most enterprise software connects to Avimark via established APIs

---

## Top 3 Weaknesses

1. **Legacy UI / no mobile / no cloud** — Windows on-premise only; no mobile access; modern workflow expectations unmet
2. **Zero native AI** — all 10 AI/Agentic features score ❌; no AI SOAP, no agentic capabilities, no predictive analytics
3. **Declining investment / maintenance-mode perception** — Covetrus actively migrating users to Pulse; community perception is sunset risk HIGH; new feature development appears minimal

---

## AI/Agentic Summary

All 10 AI/Agentic features: ❌

Avimark has no AI capabilities of any kind. The product predates modern AI tooling, and as Covetrus focuses investment on Covetrus Pulse, there is no indication that AI features will be added to Avimark. Practices seeking AI-assisted documentation, agentic workflows, or predictive analytics must look elsewhere.
