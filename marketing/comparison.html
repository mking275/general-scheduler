VPMA vs. Shepherd

Shepherd is the most advanced AI competitor in the vet PIMS market — built by veterinarians, with native TranscribeAI SOAP drafting. Here's how the two approaches differ fundamentally.

🐑
Shepherd
AI-assisted, doctor-controlled
AI Philosophy
Reactive — AI acts only when the doctor initiates. Every output requires human approval before anything is recorded.
SOAP AI
TranscribeAI listens and suggests SOAP text. Vet reviews and approves before finalizing.
Follow-up
Template-based message sends. Staff must configure and trigger.
Patient Risk
DiagnoseAI provides differential suggestions on-demand. No automated risk scoring.
Waitlist
Via Oliver integration (passive). No AI-powered backfill.
Architecture
Three siloed AI tools (TranscribeAI, SummarizeAI, DiagnoseAI). No composable agent framework.
🏥
VPMA
Proactive agentic platform
AI Philosophy
Proactive — AI agents act autonomously on routine tasks, freeing your entire team — not just the doctor in the exam room.
SOAP AI
Ambient SOAP drafting AND downstream agentic actions triggered automatically from the signed note.
Follow-up
AI agent detects post-visit need, drafts appropriate message, sends autonomously, and escalates concerns to staff.
Patient Risk
Automated risk score for every patient on every visit. High-risk patients flagged before the appointment starts.
Waitlist
AI immediately identifies and contacts the optimal waitlisted patient when a slot opens. Fills gaps in seconds.
Architecture
Modular agentic framework — composable, orchestrated pipeline. Every MOD adds new AI agents to the shared platform.
