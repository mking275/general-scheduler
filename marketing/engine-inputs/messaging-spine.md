## 1. Product & audience
- **Product:** VetAgent (VPMA) — AI-native veterinary practice management. The only PIMS with a **native AI Chief of Staff (Vera)** who proactively runs the administrative pipeline so the vet stays on medicine.
- **Market:** US, English.
- **ICP:** Independent companion-animal practice, **2–4 vets, 1 location**, currently on **Cornerstone, Avimark, or Shepherd**; $800K–$2.5M revenue; 6–14 staff.
- **Buyer:** owner-veterinarian (also practices full-time; financially conscious) — final decision, relies on the practice manager for research. Trigger: Avimark sunset, PetDesk renewal, server crash, new-practice opening, staff burnout. `[VC-7]`

**Layer 1 — Hook (why now / the displacement + the cost).**
- **"The biggest displacement wave in PIMS history is here: Cornerstone has no cloud roadmap and Avimark is being sunset — 25,000+ practices are being forced to move."** `[VC-1]`
- **"The average 3-vet practice pays ~$2,100/month across 7 disconnected tools that don't talk to each other — and the vet still charts until midnight."** `[VC-2][VC-5]`
- "Staff lose 6+ hours a week just copying data between systems." `[VC-6]`

**Layer 2 — Relief (why us / the Chief of Staff who acts).**
- **"Meet Vera. Your practice's AI Chief of Staff."** *(tagline)*
- **"Every other PIMS records what your team did. Vera acts before they have to — she runs intake, fills cancellations from your waitlist, drafts discharges, and flags at-risk patients."** `[VC-8]`
- **"Replace 7 tools with one. The typical 3-vet practice saves ~$1,405/month — $16,860 a year — on day one."** `[VC-3]`
- (category gap): **"The agentic operating layer scores ❌ across 7 of 8 PIMS platforms. The category is empty — Vera fills it."** `[VC-9]`

**Layer 3 — Journey (why bigger / it compounds).**
- **"The only PIMS that gets smarter as you use it — Vera learns your patients, your vets, your patterns, and that institutional knowledge compounds."** `[VC-10]`
- "Start by replacing the stack. Stay for a Chief of Staff who never clocks out — and always leaves the clinical call to you."

## 3. Benefits (customer language — what they GET)
1. **Stop paying for 7 tools that don't talk** — one login, one bill, ~$16,860/year back. `[VC-3]`
2. **Stop charting until midnight** — Vera reads the signed SOAP and drafts the discharge in plain English for one-click approval. `[VC-8]`
3. **Never lose a slot or let a patient quietly churn** — Vera fills cancellations from the waitlist and flags overdue patients before they're gone. `[VC-8]`

## 4. Objections → answers
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We've invested a lot in Cornerstone." | Your investment is in your *data* — and all of it migrates with you. Cornerstone has no cloud, no mobile, and zero AI (every agentic feature scores ❌). The question is whether to move to another record-keeper or to the first system that actually runs the practice. `[VC-9]` |
| "We use Avimark." | The decision's been made for you — Covetrus is sunsetting it. You have a switching event already; make it count on the platform where AI fills the schedule and chases follow-up, not just records it. `[VC-1]` |
| "We use Shepherd." | Shepherd's SOAP AI is genuinely great. The gap is everything around it — click-heavy UX, no agentic follow-up, and you still pay for PetDesk on top. VetAgent includes native comms and the autonomous layer Shepherd chose not to build. `[VC-8]` |
| "Is Vera making medical decisions?" | No. Vera handles routine admin and *proposes*; your vet approves every clinical and financial decision. She's a Chief of Staff, not a clinician. |

## 5. Voice & style rules
- **Concrete, never hype** (from the brand voice guide): *"This agent fills your waitlist without anyone asking — usually in under 4 minutes,"* NOT *"intelligent automation leverages advanced ML to optimize availability."* `[VC-11]`
- Lead with the math (real numbers), then the agentic story; respect the vet's intelligence; no false empathy.
- **Shield for the owner, deference to the clinician:** Vera takes admin off their plate; the vet keeps the medicine.
